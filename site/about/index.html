Margin.

A free PDF editor that runs entirely in your browser

Margin edits PDFs on your own device. There is nothing to install, no account, and your files are never uploaded: the PDF engine runs in the page itself.

Open Margin

What you can do

Edit text

Change existing text in place, add new text boxes with mixed fonts and sizes, find and replace, and check spelling.

Comment and mark up

Highlight, underline and strike out text; draw, add shapes, lines, arrows, callouts, notes and stamps.

Forms

Fill in PDF forms, create your own fields, or let Margin detect the fill-in lines and boxes of a printed form.

Sign

Draw your signature once and place it anywhere, on any page.

Pages

Reorder, rotate, insert, delete, crop, split and extract pages; merge several PDFs and images into one.

Scan and OCR

Turn photos or a camera scan into a PDF, and recognise the text of scanned pages so it can be searched and copied.

Compare

See what changed between two versions of a PDF, word by word or as a visual overlay.

Protect and finish

Redact, add passwords, watermarks, headers, footers and page numbers; flatten, compress and print.

Convert

Save the text as Markdown or plain text, save all images at once, and repair damaged PDFs.

Made for many languages

Text is shaped properly, with fonts made for each script: Thai, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional, with pinyin), Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Lao, Khmer and Burmese, alongside English and other Latin-script languages.

Private by design

  • Your PDFs are opened, edited and saved in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Unsaved work survives a refresh or a closed tab, stored only on your device. Passwords are never stored.
  • The site loads nothing from other domains: no trackers, no ads, no third-party fonts.
  • After the first visit (about 2.7 MB), Margin opens from your browser's cache.

Free and open source

Margin is free to use and released under the MIT licence. It is built on PDFium, the PDF engine of Chrome, compiled to WebAssembly, with HarfBuzz for text shaping. Open-source licences.

Open Margin
Margin works in current desktop and mobile browsers, on computers, tablets and phones.
